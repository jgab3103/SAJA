Live Peformance

We tend to smoosh all kinds of things together when playing live. Some jazz, some funk, some soundscape and ambient noise. Sometimes we like to pull it right back to the basics, just acoustic gutiar and voice. But other times it is messing with loops and samples, synths and computers.

Spun Treasures
